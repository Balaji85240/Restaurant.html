About Us

Tamilan Restrarunt began as a small family kitchen inspired by the flavours of Tamilnadu, Rajasthan, Punjab, and Hyderabad.

We prepare our food in small batches using fresh vegetables, quality ingredients, and hand-ground spices. Our aim is to serve every guest a warm meal that feels like home.
